# Storybook Packager Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ethan Lin** ("Publisher," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Storybook Packager (the "Application"). The project is sponsored by Excelsior University. Excelsior University is not responsible for Publisher's privacy practices solely because it sponsored the project.

Storybook Packager is a desktop authoring tool used to create and package Storybook+ presentations.

## 1. Privacy by Design

Storybook Packager is designed primarily to process project content on the user's computer.

We do not require users to create an account, and the Application does not transmit the contents of Storybook Packager projects to Publisher's servers merely because a user creates, edits, opens, saves, validates, or exports a project.

Files you create or open through the Application generally remain in locations on your computer or storage systems that you select.

The Application may be made available for free use by individuals and organizations around the world. Availability in a jurisdiction does not mean that Publisher represents the Application or this Policy satisfies every privacy, data-protection, educational, consumer, or accessibility requirement in that jurisdiction.

## 2. Information You Place in Projects

Storybook Packager may process information that you choose to place in a project, including:

- presentation titles and metadata;
- author names and biographies;
- images;
- audio and video;
- captions and transcripts;
- HTML content;
- notes and descriptions;
- XML and JSON configuration data;
- quizzes and educational content;
- downloadable materials; and
- other files selected by you.

This information may include personal information if you choose to include personal information in your project.

Unless expressly stated otherwise in this Policy, processing a file locally in Storybook Packager does not mean that Publisher receives or stores that file.

You are responsible for determining whether you have permission to use personal information belonging to another individual in content that you create or distribute.

## 3. Information Automatically Collected by the Application

Storybook Packager does not include Publisher-operated behavioral analytics, advertising tracking, or application telemetry. It does not send Publisher information about how you use the Application.

The Application also does not automatically send crash reports to Publisher.

The Application stores a small amount of local application state on your own computer, including window positions, a list of recently opened projects, and a record of your acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy (the accepted document version, the date and time of acceptance, and the application version in use). This information stays in the Application's data folder on your computer and is not transmitted to Publisher.

## 4. Software Updates and Network Requests

The current Application does not include an automatic software-update mechanism. The in-app update check currently reports that update checks are not available.

The Application may still make network requests in limited circumstances, including when you use the development build, open or preview content that references a remote resource, configure third-party content, or operate Exported Content that references third-party services. For example, previewing or distributing content that embeds externally hosted video or media may cause requests to the applicable third-party service.

Such requests may necessarily disclose technical information such as your IP address, date and time of the request, browser or Application-related information, operating-system information, or similar information to the server receiving the request.

## 5. Storybook+ Integrations Configured by Authors

Storybook Packager may allow a project author to configure Storybook+ content that references or interacts with third-party services.

Depending on the configuration selected by the author, an exported Storybook+ presentation may interact with services such as:

- analytics or audience-measurement providers;
- video-hosting providers;
- externally hosted media;
- embedded websites or HTML content; or
- other third-party resources.

These integrations belong to the exported presentation and are distinct from Publisher's own collection of information through Storybook Packager.

For example, if a project author configures analytics in a Storybook+ presentation and later publishes that presentation on a website, the resulting analytics activity may be controlled by the organization or person operating that presentation rather than by Publisher.

Project authors and presentation operators are responsible for determining whether their deployment requires notices, consent mechanisms, contracts with service providers, or other privacy measures.

## 6. Third-Party Services

If you choose to access a third-party service from the Application or configure such a service for use in Exported Content, that third party may receive information in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms.

Publisher does not control the independent privacy practices of third parties.

You should review the policies applicable to a third-party service before using or enabling it.

## 7. GitHub and Open-Source Participation
